BetterFullscreen For Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the BetterFullscreen iOS extension app ("BetterFullscreen," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information.
 

1. Introduction

BetterFullscreen is an iOS extension that enhances your web browsing experience by improving the full-screen video functionality. The extension is designed to function locally on your device and operates within the secure, isolated sandboxed environment provided by iOS.
 

2. Data Collection

BetterFullscreen does not collect any personal data.
The extension is designed with a strict privacy-first principle. It operates entirely on your local device and does not transmit any information to us or any third party. Specifically, we do not collect:
  • Your name, email address, or any other identifying information.
  • Your browsing history or a record of the websites you visit.
  • Any information about the videos you watch or your viewing habits.
  • Any data that can be used to identify you, your device, or track your activity across apps and websites.

 
3. Data Processing

Because BetterFullscreen does not collect any personal data, there is no data to process. The app's function is purely to enhance your local browsing experience. The extension only processes the necessary information to perform its function within your browser's private and secure sandbox.
 

4. Third-Party Access

BetterFullscreen does not share any data with third parties. No data is ever sold, rented, or exchanged with third-party service providers, advertisers, or data brokers.
 

5. Data Security

Since no personal data is ever collected or transmitted, there is no risk of a data breach involving your personal information. The extension operates within the secure iOS sandbox, and any functionality it performs is limited to your device. The data it interacts with remains protected by the security features of the iOS operating system.
 

6. User Rights

Because BetterFullscreen does not collect or store any personal data, there is no information for you to request access to, correct, or delete.
 

7. Children's Privacy

BetterFullscreen does not collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Given that no data is collected from any user, we are not subject to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). The app is not directed at children.
 

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated policy within the app or on the App Store listing. The "Last Updated" date at the top of the policy will be revised. We encourage you to review this policy periodically for any changes.
